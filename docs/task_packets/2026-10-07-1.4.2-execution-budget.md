## Dependency map and constraints

Portable role contracts own execution and review behavior. The Copilot addendum owns routing; delegation guidance supplies compact handoff content; checkpoints persist resumable state; bounded-execution guidance defines policy and release metadata exposes the change. Consumer manuals, overlays, domain safeguards, actual validation commands and local finalization controls remain authoritative.

The policy must keep repository custom-agent availability, model selection, and runtime tool enforcement distinct. Repository custom agents are the main path: their adapters direct the worker to read portable/local Markdown in place. Generic fallback must use role/packet paths and compact deltas rather than copied unchanged authority bodies. A missing coder report permits one evidence-recovery pass only, not another implementation loop. After a reviewed correction, review is limited to the repair delta and affected dependencies unless authority, scope, candidate, target or material evidence drifts.

## Acceptance criteria

1. New packets have a clear default execution/recovery/review budget while explicit limits and stronger consumer controls prevail.
2. A missing or incomplete coder evidence envelope permits exactly one bounded recovery pass and never silently authorizes another implementation attempt or rediscovery loop.
3. Generic handoffs reference exact workspace authorities and supply only changed scope/candidate/evidence/budget details; unchanged manuals, packets and role bodies are not recopied.
4. Post-repair review is delta-and-affected-dependency by default; full refresh remains required on authority, scope, candidate, target or material-evidence drift.
5. Allowance exhaustion persists a compact checkpoint and requests owner direction; it cannot reset budget or trigger silent replacement workers.
6. Registration, model selection and runtime tool enforcement remain distinct and no runtime/token enforcement is claimed.
7. The standards describe repository-defined workspace custom agents as the normal Copilot route and generic role-guided sessions as a bounded fallback; they do not claim platform-wide standard agents exist.
8. Version, README, changelog and manifest are internally consistent for candidate `1.4.2`; later publication evidence is recorded only after the reviewed commit is merged and tagged.
9. Structural validation and disposable fixtures pass, with limitations reported honestly.
